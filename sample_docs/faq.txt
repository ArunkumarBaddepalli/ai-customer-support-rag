Pizza Palace — Frequently Asked Questions

Timings:
We are open every day from 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends and public holidays. Kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing time for last orders.

Menu:
We serve Margherita, Farmhouse, Peppy Paneer, Chicken Tikka, and Pepperoni pizzas in Small, Medium, and Large sizes. We also serve garlic bread, cheesy fries, cold drinks, and a choice of three dips: cheese, mayo, and peri-peri.

Prices:
Margherita: Small ₹149, Medium ₹249, Large ₹349.
Farmhouse: Small ₹199, Medium ₹299, Large ₹399.
Peppy Paneer: Small ₹199, Medium ₹319, Large ₹419.
Chicken Tikka: Small ₹249, Medium ₹379, Large ₹479.
Pepperoni: Small ₹229, Medium ₹359, Large ₹459.
Garlic bread: ₹99. Cheesy fries: ₹129. Cold drinks: ₹49.

Delivery:
We deliver within a 7 km radius. Delivery usually takes 30-40 minutes. Delivery is free for orders above ₹399; otherwise a flat ₹40 delivery fee applies. You can track your order live once it is out for delivery.

Payment:
We accept cash on delivery, UPI, and all major credit/debit cards. Payment is not accepted in installments.

Returns and refunds:
If your order arrives cold, incorrect, or damaged, report it within 30 minutes of delivery to get a free replacement or a full refund automatically. Reports made between 30 minutes and 2 hours after delivery are still accepted, but are reviewed case by case. The final deadline to report any problem with an order is 2 hours after delivery; we cannot help after that. We also cannot offer refunds for change-of-mind cancellations once the order is out for delivery.

Cancellations:
Orders can be cancelled for a full refund only if the kitchen has not yet started preparing them, usually within 5 minutes of placing the order.

Offers:
Buy 1 Get 1 free on all Medium pizzas every Tuesday. Students get a flat 15% discount with a valid student ID shown at delivery.

Contact:
For any other queries, call our support line at +91-98765-43210 or email support@pizzapalace.example between 10 AM and 10 PM.
