Bloom & Stem — Customer FAQ

Opening hours:
We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We are closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Location:
Our shop is at 42 Garden Lane, Riverside. Street parking is free for the first hour.

Bouquets and prices:
Classic Rose bouquet: small $35, medium $55, large $80.
Seasonal Mixed bouquet: small $28, medium $45, large $65.
Sunflower bouquet: small $30, medium $48, large $70.
Orchid arrangement: $75.
Dried flower bouquet: $40.
A hand-tied wrap is included. Ceramic vases are an extra $15.

Same-day delivery:
Order before 1:00 PM for same-day delivery within Riverside. Orders after 1:00 PM are delivered the next day. Delivery is $8, or free on orders over $60.

Delivery area:
We deliver within 15 km of the shop. We cannot deliver outside this area, but you can collect from the shop instead.

Weddings and events:
We take wedding bookings with at least 6 weeks' notice. A consultation is free and takes about an hour. A 30% deposit secures your date and is non-refundable within 14 days of the event.

Subscriptions:
Weekly subscription: $45 per delivery. Fortnightly: $50 per delivery. Monthly: $60 per delivery. You can pause or cancel any time before the next dispatch date with no fee.

Flower care:
Trim 2 cm off the stems at an angle and change the water every two days. Keep arrangements out of direct sunlight and away from ripening fruit. Most bouquets last 5 to 7 days.

Freshness guarantee:
If your flowers wilt within 3 days, send us a photo and we will replace them free of charge.

Cancellations and refunds:
Cancel before 1:00 PM on the day of delivery for a full refund. Once a bouquet is out for delivery we cannot cancel it. Wedding deposits are non-refundable within 14 days of the event.

Payment:
We accept all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and cash in store. We do not accept cheques.

Contact:
Call 555-0142 or email hello@bloomandstem.example between 9 AM and 6 PM.
