Riverside Family Clinic — Patient FAQ

Emergencies:
This clinic does not handle emergencies. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms or any life-threatening problem, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a clinic appointment and do not use this chat for urgent medical problems.

Opening hours:
Monday to Friday 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Closed Sundays and public holidays. The last appointment of each day is booked 30 minutes before closing. Phone lines open at 7:45 AM.

Booking an appointment:
Book through the patient portal, by phone on (555) 240-1180, or in person at reception. Routine appointments are usually available within 3 working days. Same-day urgent slots open at 8:00 AM each morning and are allocated first come, first served. A standard consultation is 15 minutes; ask for a double appointment if you have more than two issues to discuss.

Cancelling or rescheduling:
Give at least 4 hours' notice through the portal or by phone. Missed appointments without notice incur a $25 no-show fee, waived once per calendar year. Three no-shows in 12 months may mean appointments have to be booked by phone only.

Fees:
Standard consultation $75. Extended consultation $130. Annual health check $190. Vaccination administration fee $20 plus the cost of the vaccine. Minor procedures from $150. Medical certificate $30. Travel health consultation $95. Fees are payable on the day by card, cash or bank transfer.

Insurance and billing:
We bill Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna and United Healthcare directly. Bring your card to every visit. For any other insurer you pay on the day and we provide an itemised receipt to claim back. Copays are collected at check-in. Billing questions go to billing@riversideclinic.example.

Repeat prescriptions:
Request repeats through the portal or by dropping the slip at reception. Allow 2 working days. We do not take repeat requests over the phone, because transcription errors on medication names are a safety risk. Controlled medications require an appointment and cannot be issued as a repeat.

Test results:
Blood test results are normally back within 3 working days, imaging within 5. Results appear in the portal once a doctor has reviewed them. We contact you directly if anything needs action. No news is not confirmation of a normal result — check the portal or call if you have not heard within 7 days.

Referrals:
Specialist referrals are issued during a consultation and sent electronically the same day. You will receive a copy in the portal. If the specialist's office has not contacted you within 10 working days, call us and we will follow up.

Medical records:
Request a copy of your records in writing or through the portal. Records are provided within 30 days at no charge for the first request each year; additional copies cost $15. Records are released only to the patient, or to a third party with written signed consent.

Registering as a new patient:
Bring photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of current medications. Registration takes about 10 minutes and can be completed online in advance. We accept new patients living within 15 miles of the clinic.

Home visits:
Available for registered patients who are housebound, Monday to Friday afternoons only. Request before 10:00 AM on the day. A home visit is $160.

Location and parking:
42 Riverside Avenue, on the corner of Mill Street. Free patient parking for 90 minutes in the rear lot, entrance from Mill Street. Two accessible bays by the main door. Bus routes 6 and 19 stop outside.

Accessibility:
Step-free access throughout, accessible toilet, and a hearing loop at reception. Tell us when booking if you need an interpreter — we can arrange one at no cost with 2 working days' notice.

Contact:
Phone (555) 240-1180, Monday to Friday 7:45 AM to 6:00 PM. Email hello@riversideclinic.example — we reply within 1 working day. Email is not monitored outside opening hours and must never be used for urgent problems.
