CityLab Diagnostics — Patient FAQ

Emergencies:
CityLab is a testing laboratory, not an urgent care centre. We do not diagnose, treat, or interpret results over chat or phone. If you feel seriously unwell, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Always discuss what your results mean with the doctor who ordered the test.

Opening hours:
Sample collection runs Monday to Saturday, 6:30 AM to 2:00 PM. The reception desk stays open until 6:00 PM for report collection and payments. Closed Sundays and public holidays. Fasting tests are taken between 6:30 AM and 10:00 AM only.

Do I need an appointment:
Walk-ins are accepted for routine blood tests. Appointments are required for glucose tolerance tests, 24-hour urine collection, and any test needing special preparation. Book on the portal or call (555) 907-3355. Average walk-in wait is 20 minutes; Monday mornings are the busiest.

Do I need a doctor's prescription:
Most tests can be booked without one. A prescription is required for controlled panels and for any test you intend to claim on insurance. Results for prescribed tests are sent to your doctor as well as to you.

Fasting instructions:
Fasting means no food and no drinks other than plain water for the stated period. Continue prescribed medication unless the doctor who ordered the test said otherwise. Fasting is 10 to 12 hours for a lipid profile, 8 hours for fasting glucose, and 12 hours for an insulin panel. No fasting is needed for a complete blood count, thyroid panel, or HbA1c.

Test prices:
Complete Blood Count $18. Lipid Profile $34. Fasting Blood Glucose $12. HbA1c $29. Thyroid Panel (TSH, T3, T4) $42. Liver Function Panel $38. Kidney Function Panel $36. Vitamin D $55. Vitamin B12 $48. Urine Routine $15. Full Body Checkup package (62 parameters) $149.

Home sample collection:
Available within 12 miles of the lab, Monday to Saturday 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM. The home collection fee is $15, waived on orders over $100. Book by 8:00 PM the previous evening. Our phlebotomist carries photo ID — always ask to see it before letting anyone in.

Turnaround times:
Routine blood tests are reported the same day if the sample is received before 11:00 AM. Thyroid and vitamin panels take 1 working day. Culture and sensitivity tests take 3 to 5 working days because the sample has to grow. Histopathology takes 5 to 7 working days.

Getting your report:
Reports are published to the patient portal and emailed as a PDF. Printed copies can be collected from reception until 6:00 PM with photo ID, or by someone else carrying your written authorisation. There is no charge for the first printed copy; reprints are $3.

Abnormal or critical results:
Critical values are phoned to the ordering doctor immediately and flagged in your report. Our staff cannot tell you what a result means or what to do about it — that conversation belongs with your doctor, who knows your history.

Repeat and retest policy:
If a sample is rejected for clotting, insufficient volume, or transport delay, we recollect free of charge and tell you the same day. If you want a test repeated for your own reassurance, it is charged at the normal price.

Insurance and payment:
We bill Aetna, Cigna and Blue Cross Blue Shield directly for prescribed tests. Self-requested tests are not covered by any insurer and are paid on the day. We accept card, cash, and UPI. Itemised receipts are issued automatically for claims.

Children and infants:
We collect samples from children of all ages. A parent or guardian must be present and bring the child's ID or birth certificate. Paediatric collection uses smaller-volume tubes. Book ahead for infants under 12 months.

Accreditation:
CityLab is CAP accredited and CLIA certified, and takes part in external quality assessment every quarter. Accreditation numbers are printed on the footer of every report.

Contact:
