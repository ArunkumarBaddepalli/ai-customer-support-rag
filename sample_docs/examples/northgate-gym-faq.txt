Northgate Fitness — Member FAQ

Opening hours:
The gym is open 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM Monday to Friday, and 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekends and public holidays. Members with a 24/7 plan can enter any time using their key fob.

Membership plans:
Off-Peak (weekdays before 4 PM): $29 per month.
Standard: $45 per month.
24/7 Access: $59 per month.
Student (with valid ID): $32 per month.
Annual Standard paid upfront: $450, which saves two months.
There is a one-off joining fee of $25, waived if you join during a promotion.

Contracts and cancellation:
All monthly plans are rolling with no minimum term. Cancel by giving 30 days' notice through the app or at reception. Annual plans can be cancelled early only for medical reasons with a doctor's note, and are refunded pro rata.

Freezing your membership:
You can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year at $10 per month. Freezes must be requested at least 5 days before your billing date.

Classes:
Included with Standard and 24/7 plans: spin, yoga, HIIT, pilates and boxing. Off-Peak members can attend classes before 4 PM only. Book up to 7 days ahead in the app. Classes cancelled less than 2 hours before the start time incur a $5 no-show fee.

Personal training:
Single session: $60. Five sessions: $270. Ten sessions: $500. Sessions expire 6 months after purchase. Cancel with 24 hours' notice or the session is forfeited.

Facilities:
Free weights, resistance machines, cardio floor, functional training rig, sauna, and changing rooms with lockers. Bring your own padlock, or buy one at reception for $8. Towel hire is $2.

Guest policy:
Members can bring a guest for $12 per visit. Guests must be 16 or over and complete a health form on arrival.

Age requirements:
Members must be 16 or over. Members aged 16 and 17 need a parent or guardian to sign the registration form and cannot use the free weights area unsupervised.

Induction:
Every new member gets a free 45-minute induction covering equipment use and a basic programme. Book it at reception or in the app.

Lost key fob:
Report it immediately so we can deactivate it. A replacement costs $10.

Contact:
Call 555-0188 or email members@northgatefitness.example. Reception is staffed 8 AM to 8 PM daily.
