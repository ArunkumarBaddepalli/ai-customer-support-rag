PixelCart — Online Store Help

About us:
PixelCart sells refurbished laptops, phones and accessories. Every device is tested, cleaned and comes with a warranty.

Shipping:
Standard shipping is $5 and takes 3 to 5 working days. Express is $12 and takes 1 to 2 working days. Shipping is free on orders over $150. We dispatch Monday to Friday; orders placed after 2 PM ship the next working day.

International shipping:
We ship to Canada and Mexico. International delivery takes 7 to 14 working days and costs $25. Customers are responsible for any customs duties.

Order tracking:
You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. If it hasn't arrived within 10 working days of dispatch, contact us and we'll open an investigation with the carrier.

Warranty:
All refurbished laptops and phones come with a 12-month warranty. Accessories carry a 6-month warranty. The warranty covers hardware faults but not accidental damage, liquid damage, or normal battery wear.

Battery health:
Every refurbished phone ships with a battery at 85% health or better. If yours arrives below 85%, we'll replace the battery free.

Grading:
Grade A means excellent, with no visible marks. Grade B has light scratches not visible in use. Grade C has visible cosmetic wear but works perfectly. Grading affects appearance only, never function.

Returns:
Return anything within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided it's in the condition you received it. Start a return from your account page and we'll email a prepaid label. Refunds are processed within 5 working days of the item arriving back.

Faulty items:
If an item develops a fault under warranty, contact us with your order number and a description. We'll repair it, replace it, or refund you. Return shipping for faulty items is free.

Payment:
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We also offer interest-free payment in 4 instalments on orders over $200.

Data on returned devices:
All devices are fully wiped and factory reset before resale. Please back up and sign out of your own accounts before returning a device to us.

Order changes:
You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, from your account page. After that the order is usually already being picked and cannot be changed.

Contact:
Email help@pixelcart.example or call 555-0170, Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM. We reply to emails within one working day.
